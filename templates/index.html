Rec League Manager

Seasons, teams, rosters, schedules, referees, and results for one rec soccer league.

What you can do

  1. Search players by name or email, then open a player to see their teams and stats.
  2. View a team to see its roster and its full schedule with results.
  3. Add a player to a roster with a jersey number. Open a team and click "Add player to roster".
  4. Reschedule or cancel a game. Open an upcoming game from Games and click "Reschedule or cancel".
  5. See standings and top scorers for any season.
  6. Enter a final score and player stats. Open an upcoming game and click "Enter final score".

You can also schedule a new game in an open time slot, and assign referees from any upcoming game's page.
